Privacy Policy

How EchoLogicer handles information across websites, iOS software, support systems, and advertising operations.

This Privacy Policy explains how EchoLogicer may collect, use, store, disclose, and protect information when users interact with our websites, iOS software products, support channels, analytics systems, and IAA-supported services.

Policy Scope

Applies to the public website, product support channels, iOS releases, and monetization-related service infrastructure.

Because EchoLogicer works on iOS software development and IAA monetization systems, this policy covers both direct website interactions and operational data that may be processed through apps, analytics, advertising, and support flows.

Section 01

Information We Collect

We may collect information you submit directly, such as contact emails, support requests, issue descriptions, screenshots, communication history, and product inquiry details.

We may also receive technical information including device model, operating system version, app version, crash or diagnostic information, country or region, language, approximate usage timing, and product interaction signals.

Section 02

How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and improve our websites, respond to support needs, review business inquiries, maintain product quality, investigate technical issues, and communicate about services or releases.

We may also use operational data to understand product performance, refine IAA monetization design, improve retention behavior, and maintain healthy release infrastructure for ad-supported mobile products.

Section 03

IAA Advertising and Analytics

Some EchoLogicer-supported products may use IAA advertising monetization. In those cases, advertising partners, mediation providers, and analytics platforms may process identifiers, ad interaction data, device characteristics, event-level product signals, and performance information.

These systems may be used to deliver ads, measure campaign effectiveness, improve revenue efficiency, analyze retention, prevent fraud, and support operational optimization.

Section 04

Device Permissions

Our iOS software products may request permissions relevant to the product experience, such as notifications, media access, storage-related functionality, or other device-level capabilities where needed for the service.

Users can typically manage permissions through iOS settings. Restricting permissions may change or limit some product behaviors.

Section 05

Third-Party Services

EchoLogicer may work with third-party providers for hosting, analytics, crash reporting, customer support routing, advertising delivery, revenue mediation, operational communication, and infrastructure services.

These providers may process data on our behalf or as part of their own service functions, subject to their policies and applicable agreements.

Section 06

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, support release operations, investigate technical problems, comply with legal obligations, and protect business integrity.

Retention periods vary based on the data category, operational purpose, legal context, and whether the information relates to support, monetization, diagnostics, analytics, or public site communication.

Section 07

International Transfers

Because our providers or technical infrastructure may operate in different jurisdictions, information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. Where appropriate, we take reasonable steps to support compliant data handling and operational safeguards.

Section 08

Security

We use commercially reasonable measures intended to help protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No online environment can be guaranteed completely secure, so absolute security cannot be promised.

Section 09

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights related to access, deletion, correction, restriction, objection, portability, or consent withdrawal. Requests can be sent using the contact details provided on this website.

Section 10

Children's Privacy

EchoLogicer does not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner that violates applicable law. If you believe data has been submitted improperly, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Section 11

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product changes, provider changes, legal requirements, or operational developments. The latest version published on this website should be treated as the current policy.

Section 12

Contact About Privacy

Privacy-related questions may be sent through our public contact routes, including support@echologicer.com, business@echologicer.com, and vip@echologicer.com.
